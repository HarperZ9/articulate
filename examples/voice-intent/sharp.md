The county cut the night ferry on 4 March 2025, and its own report lists 212 late departures that quarter. The board voted 4 to 1. The crews say they were short-staffed for a year before the cut.

The county says the route lost money. From the board's side that holds: a boat with eleven riders at 1 a.m. is hard to defend in a budget meeting. But I think the late count was a staffing problem, and the cut answered a different question.

What if the county had hired twenty deckhands in place of the cut? Would you still be at the dock?
