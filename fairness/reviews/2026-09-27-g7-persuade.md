## The question

The strict profiles block one in seven school essays by writers not recorded as
English learners. Which rules drive that, and does each blocked finding carry
the reader cost its reason states in these essays? The G2 rows name the rules
that fire more often on those essays: wordiness, marketing superlatives,
throat-clearing openers, uncited appeals to studies, stacked hedges and four
rules that never fired on a learner essay.

## Who reviews, and what the review can decide

The maintainer, with the second reader the pull request 9 checklist still asks
for once one is found. The review reads the rule reasons against the blocked
counts in the receipt; it reads no essay text into the repository. What it
decides becomes part of the next ruleset, and that ruleset is confirmed on a
corpus pre-registered for it, since this corpus has been read.
