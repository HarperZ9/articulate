In a realm where the world is but a single, shimmering plane, the inhabitants of this unique plane have no concept of depth or height. To them, everything is as flat and unyielding as the bed of a calm lake. The vast, luminous expanse they call "above" is but a horizon, a shimmering edge that touches the edges of their world without ever rising or sinking. They perceive all that exists as a seamless plane, undulating with subtle variations of light, but never rising to the level of depth. 

This lack of three-dimensional space doesn’t hinder their daily lives as much as it shapes their understanding of themselves and their world. In their society, every structure is flat and seamless, with every surface a mirror, reflecting back not just the immediate surroundings but also the infinite plane in which they dwell. Every form, from the smallest insect to the tallest tree, is perfectly flat. They are, in a way, more aware of the plane than they are of any particular form within it. 

They move with a grace that is both elegant and fluid, their steps never sinking into the "earth," but never floating above it either. Their conversations are as flat and unencumbered as the land, with no room for the intricacies of depth or complexity that are so integral to the human experience in three dimensions. The air is filled with the chatter of their voices, each word a flat, crisp sound that reverberates across the plane, often echoing in a way that mimics the way light travels, bending around corners and following lines without ever disappearing.

In this flat world, colors are a constant presence, like the hues of a painting stretched out before them, infinite in their variation across the plane. They see the sky as a gradient of light, with no horizon to demarcate the sky from the land. The sun casts its light, not as a point in the sky, but as a glowing circle that stretches across the plane, casting shadows that are also flat, lines that cross this infinite canvas without ever changing their form or direction.

Their joy and sorrow, their happiness and sorrows, manifest as waves of sentiment rippling through the plane, a vast, unbroken expanse where the smallest emotion can spread and dissipate, touching every point in the endless landscape. The very fabric of their reality is a testament to the simplicity and simplicity of their existence, a world without depth that is both beautiful and profoundly unremarkable.
