Send your form before the review starts. We use what you send to check if you
can get the grant. If something is missing, we will ask you for it. We may ask
for more proof after the first check. That can happen when your papers do not
match. If you cannot show that you meet the rules, we will write to tell you. We
will not look at a form until it is complete. Keep a copy of each page you send.
Call us if you have a question. The phone line is open from 9 to 5 on weekdays.
You can also write to us. Most people hear back within two weeks. Some forms
take longer when we need to ask a second office. We will tell you if that
happens to yours.
