In order to facilitate the timely processing of applications, applicants are
required to submit all documentation prior to the commencement of the evaluation
period. Notwithstanding any previous correspondence, the department will utilize
the information provided for the purpose of determining eligibility, and
additional verification procedures may be implemented subsequent to the initial
determination in circumstances where the documentation submitted is considered
insufficient or inconsistent with departmental requirements. Applicants who are
unable to demonstrate compliance with the established administrative criteria
will be notified by the responsible office. Consideration of incomplete
applications will be deferred until such time as the necessary supplementary
documentation has been received, reviewed, and authenticated by administrative
personnel.
